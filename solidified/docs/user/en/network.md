# Network Stream

The Network (`/network`) shows a live feed of everything your connections have posted — the social heart of your Hubzilla experience.

[IMAGE: Network page with filter bar and post list visible]

## View Modes

Use the view switcher (top-right of the filter bar, or bottom row on mobile) to change how posts are displayed:

| Mode | Description |
|------|-------------|
| **Feed** | Full posts in a vertical feed, one below the other |
| **Grid** (masonry) | Posts arranged in a Pinterest-style card grid |
| **List** | Compact single-line list — good for quick scanning |
| **Inbox** | Conversation-style view grouping threads together |

## Sort Order

The sort control in the filter bar decides what order the stream comes back in.
On a wide screen it's a row of tabs; on a phone it collapses into a single
button that opens the same list.

There are seven orders, and they fall into two groups.

### By time

- **Latest** — newest posts first, by when they were written. The default.
- **Active** — threads with the most recent reply first. A conversation from
  last week that someone just commented on jumps to the top. Good for keeping
  up with discussions rather than announcements.
- **Unthreaded** — a flat, chronological list of *every* activity, not just
  posts: comments, likes and reactions appear as their own entries instead of
  being folded into their thread. Useful when you want to see everything that
  happened, in the order it happened.

### By popularity

These four rank posts against each other instead of putting them in time order.

- **Top** — the most liked posts, most likes first. A like counts once per
  person no matter how many times it reaches your hub.
- **Hot** — a blend of likes and freshness, so a busy stream doesn't just show
  you the same all-time favourites. Likes count on a sliding scale — going from
  1 like to 10 helps as much as going from 10 to 100 — and a post also gains
  ground simply for being newer, at a rate of one "step" every 12½ hours. The
  practical effect: **a post needs about ten times the likes to beat one posted
  half a day later.** Old posts don't sink over time; new ones just start
  higher.
- **Most discussed** — the biggest conversations, by number of replies. This is
  about *volume*, where Active is about *recency*: a 200-comment argument from
  Tuesday ranks above a thread that got one reply a minute ago.
- **Controversial** — posts that got a lot of reaction *and* a split verdict.
  A post needs both: 50 likes and 50 dislikes ranks highly, 100 likes and no
  dislikes doesn't rank at all, because everybody agreed. The more one-sided
  the split, the further it falls.

### Time range

Top, Most discussed and Controversial keep counting forever, so without a limit
they'd show you the same all-time winners on every visit. Picking one of them
opens a small **Today / Week / Month / Year / All time** menu, and the window
you choose becomes part of the label — *Most discussed (Week)*. Click the
selected tab again to change it.

They start on **Today**, so you get what's popular right now rather than the
same all-time favourites on every visit. Widen the window whenever you want to
look further back.

Hot has no time range because its freshness bonus already is one.

### Two things to know

- **They can lag a few minutes.** Ranking a whole stream is expensive, so the
  result is reused for up to 15 minutes. A post you just liked may take that
  long to climb. The time-based orders are always live.
- **Live updates pause.** The "new posts" banner only appears under Latest,
  Active and Unthreaded. Under the popularity orders, "newest" isn't the top of
  the list, so there's nothing sensible to add there — switch back to a
  time-based order to resume.
- **Sorting respects your filters.** Whatever tags, folders, connections, dates
  or searches are active, the sort applies within those results, not to the
  whole stream.

## Quick Filter Toggles

These icon buttons are always visible in the filter bar. They can be combined freely.

| Button | What it shows |
|--------|--------------|
| ★ Star | Only your starred posts |
| 🔔 Following | Posts in threads you are following |
| 👤 Conversations | Threads where you have commented, liked, or reacted |
| ✉ DMs | Direct messages only |
| 📅 Events | Event posts only |

Click a toggle again to turn it off. An active toggle is highlighted in accent colour.

[IMAGE: Filter bar with a couple of toggles active]

## Filtering by Connection

1. Click the **person icon** (or the active connection chip if one is already selected).
2. Start typing a name — a suggestion list appears.
3. Select a connection to see only their posts.
4. Click the × on the chip to clear the filter.

[IMAGE: Connection filter with typeahead dropdown open]

## Search

Click the **magnifying glass** to open the search field.

- Type to search post text — results update as you type (400 ms delay).
- **Paste a URL** to import a post from a remote server — press Enter to fetch it.

[IMAGE: Search field expanded with URL pasted]

## Advanced Filters

Click the **filter icon** (≡ with lines) to open the advanced filter panel:

| Filter | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| **Tag** | Show only posts containing a specific hashtag |
| **From date** | Show posts created on or after this date |
| **To date** | Show posts created on or before this date |
| **Affinity min/max** | Filter by your affinity score with the author (0–100 scale) |

A small dot on the filter icon indicates that advanced filters are active.

[IMAGE: Advanced filter panel expanded showing all fields]

## Clear All Filters

When any filter is active, a **× button** appears at the right end of the filter bar. Click it to reset all filters and return to the default view.

## New Posts Banner

If new posts arrive while you are reading, a floating button appears at the top of the page:

> ↑ 3 new posts

Click it to load the new posts at the top of your stream.

## Load More

Scroll to the bottom — if there are more posts, a **Load more** button appears. Click it to fetch the next page. When you have seen everything, you'll see "You're all caught up".

[IMAGE: Load more button at the bottom of the stream]

## Refreshing

Click the **↺ refresh icon** at the left of the filter bar to reload the stream with the current filters.

## Stream Filters Widget

Sidebar version of the quick filter toggles, folders, tags, date range, and affinity slider described above — shown only to you (not visitors), so you can filter your Network stream without opening the panel on the page itself.

## Saved Searches Widget

Save your current combination of filters as a named search, then reapply it later with one click from this widget. Only visible to you.
