# Navigation

Hubzilla's navigation lives in three places: the primary nav list (the left sidebar on desktop, or the bottom bar and app drawer on mobile), the account/utility row beneath it, and a couple of dedicated buttons on mobile. This page explains what each link or button does — for how to actually use what's behind it, see that app's own help page.

## Hq

Your personal home base after logging in — a dashboard combining your composer, upcoming events, recent messages, and drafts in one place.

## Network

Your stream: posts from everyone you're connected to, with filters for stars, mentions, direct messages, saved searches, and more.

## Inbox

A mail-style reader for your messages, direct conversations, starred posts and filed folders — and the one place that keeps a full local copy for reading offline.

## Channel

Your own channel wall and profile — what visitors see when they view your page.

## Photos

Your photo albums and the image viewer.

## Files

Cloud file storage for uploading, organizing, and sharing files.

## Articles

The Articles app, for writing and reading long-form content.

## Wiki

Collaborative wiki pages you and your connections can edit.

## Calendar

Your calendar and events, including RSVPs.

## Chat

Chatrooms you've joined or created. A count on the item shows how many of your own rooms, and your bookmarked rooms, have unread messages.

## Webpages

Static pages you build and attach to your channel.

## Notepad

A private, personal scratchpad only you can see.

## Bookmarks

Posts and pages you've bookmarked for later.

## Directory

Search and browse other channels on this site and across the network.

## Profile

Your profile details — the "about" information visitors see on your channel.

## Cart

Your shopping cart, if this site has the Cart app enabled.

## Games

Puzzle games built into Hubzilla.

## Tools

Miscellaneous utilities bundled with your account.

## Pubstream

The public stream — public posts from across the site, visible without logging in.

## Siteinfo

Information about this Hubzilla site: version, admin contact, and terms.

## Help

Opens the help guide's table of contents.

## Admin

Site administration. Only visible to site admins.

## Settings

All of your account settings: display, profile, privacy, notifications, and more.

## Manage

Switch between the channels you own, if you manage more than one.

## Login

Sign in to your account.

## Logout

Sign out of your account.

## Register

Create a new account on this site.

## Remote Login

Log in to your home site from this one, so you can interact here using your own identity.

## Account Menu

Desktop: click your avatar and name to reveal account actions. Mobile: the account icon on the left of the bottom bar opens the same menu (settings, channel management, logout).

## Language

Switch the interface language.

## Theme

Switch between light, dark, and other color themes.

## Help Mode

Toggles help mode. While it's on, click anything on the page — a nav link, a widget, a button — to see what it does.

## Apps

Mobile only. Opens the app drawer: the site brand at the top, then your pinned apps (always expanded), then every other app grouped into collapsible Social, Productivity and System sections. The utility icons (language, theme, help, site info) sit directly under the brand, and the search box is pinned at the bottom of the drawer — it filters the whole list as you type. Account actions live in the account menu on the bottom bar.

## Favorites

Mobile only. Opens a quick menu of the apps you have pinned, without opening the full app drawer. Pin or unpin apps in Settings → Integrations.

## Actions

Mobile only. The bottom bar button is labelled with the current page's name (for example "Calendar") and opens that page's actions — the controls that appear on the right of the page header on desktop (for example Calendar's "New event" and Month/Week/Day switcher), so the header itself stays clean on a phone.

When the page has no actions of its own, the same button becomes **Compose** (pencil icon) and offers quick compose: new post, message, article, card, wiki/page or calendar event, depending on which apps are installed.

## Panel

Opens the right-hand sidebar panel — the widgets you'd otherwise see beside the page on desktop.
