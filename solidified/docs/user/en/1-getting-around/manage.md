# Manage Channels

One account can run several channels: a personal one, a group, a project page,
and so on. **Manage** (in the account menu) lists them all.

## Switching Channels

Click **Switch** on a channel to start using it. The page reloads as that
channel. The one you're using now has a **current** badge.

## Default Channel

**Make default** picks the channel you're logged into when you sign in. It has
a **default** badge.

## Creating a Channel

**+ New channel** creates another channel on this account. The counter at the
top shows how many channels you've used out of what your hub allows.

## Delegated Channels

**Delegated channels** belong to other people who have let you manage them.
Click **delegate →** to act as that channel.
