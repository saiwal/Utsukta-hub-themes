# Search and jump

One search box finds everything you've written or saved: wiki pages, articles, cards, notes, posts, files and bookmarks. The same box also gets you to any app, so you can move around without touching the menu.

## Opening it

| Where | How |
|-------|-----|
| **Desktop** | Press **Ctrl+K** (**⌘K** on a Mac), or click the magnifier in the icon row under the left menu. Press the same keys again, or **Esc**, to close it. |
| **Phone or tablet** | Open the app drawer from the bottom bar and type in the search box at the bottom of the sheet. |

## What you get

As you type, results come back in groups:

1. **Go to**: apps whose name matches, such as *Calendar* or *Wiki*. With nothing typed yet, this lists your first few apps, so the box doubles as a quick launcher. (On mobile, matching apps appear as tiles, the same as in the drawer.)
2. **Your content**: matches grouped by kind (wiki pages, articles, cards, notes, posts, files, bookmarks), newest first, with a short excerpt around the word you searched for.

Search starts once you've typed two letters. It looks in titles and text, and for files, in the file name.

**Posts** covers everything in your stream, not only what you wrote: your own posts and comments, plus whatever your connections have sent you.

## Moving with the keyboard

| Key | Does |
|-----|------|
| **↑ / ↓** | Move the highlight |
| **Enter** | Open the highlighted result |
| **Ctrl/⌘+click** | Open a result in a new tab |
| **Esc** | Close |

## Filters

Click the **Filters** button (the sliders icon) next to the search box:

- **Type**: tap one or more kinds to search only those. **All** clears the selection.
- **Tag**: show only items carrying a hashtag or category (with or without the `#`). A tag works even with no search word, so `#recipes` on its own lists everything tagged recipes. Files and bookmarks have no tags, so they're left out while a tag is set.
- **From / To**: limit results to a date range. Bookmarks have no date, so they're left out while a date is set.

## Where results open

- Wiki pages, articles, cards and posts open on their own page.
- A **note** opens the Notepad showing just that note. Click **Notepad** in the menu to see all your notes again.
- A **file** opens its folder in Files.
- A **bookmark** opens the bookmarked page. Bookmarks to other websites open in a new tab.

## Who can use it

App jumping works for everyone. Content search is only for people signed in to their own channel on this hub, and it searches only your own channel. Visitors from other hubs can still use the box to jump between apps.
