# Excalidraw

<!-- excalidraw -->

Excalidraw is a hand-drawn-style whiteboard for sketching diagrams, quick illustrations, or annotated screenshots. It's an optional feature — enable it in **Settings → Integrations** if you don't see it yet.

[IMAGE: Excalidraw canvas with a simple sketch, toolbar visible]

## Drawing from the Post Composer

Open the composer's formatting toolbar and click the **Excalidraw** button (a small sketch icon) to open the drawing canvas in a popup.

- Draw using Excalidraw's own tools (shapes, arrows, freehand, text, etc.).
- Click **Insert** to export your drawing as an image and add it to your post. Uploading may take a moment for larger drawings.
- Click **Cancel** or press **Esc** to close without inserting anything.

<!-- excalidraw_tool -->

## Excalidraw in Tools

The **Tools** app also has a standalone Excalidraw canvas (Tools → Excalidraw) for sketching without starting a post — draw, then click **Download** to save the result as a PNG to your device.

## Enabling or Disabling

Excalidraw is a frontend-only feature (it doesn't need a matching Hubzilla app). Toggle it on or off from **Settings → Integrations**; turning it off removes both the composer toolbar button and the Tools entry.
