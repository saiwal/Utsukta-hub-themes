# Bookmarks

Bookmarks keeps the links you want to come back to, sorted into folders. Open
**Bookmarks** from the sidebar.

## Saving a Bookmark

- From a post: links in a post get a small bookmark button beside them. Click it to save that link.
- By hand: click **Add bookmark**, paste the **Link**, give it a **Title**, and pick a **Folder**. Leave the folder empty and type a name under **New folder** to start a new one.
- From a chatroom: bookmarking a room adds it to your **Bookmarked Rooms** as well.

## Folders

Every folder is a card on the page. Use the folder's menu to **Rename folder**
or **Delete folder** (this deletes everything in it). **Move up** and **Move
down** change the order of the folders. The **Default folder** is where new
bookmarks go when you don't pick one.

## Editing and Removing

Each bookmark has **Visit**, **Edit** and **Remove**. In **Edit** you can change
the title and link, or **Move to** another folder.

## Your Bookmarks and Your Connections' Bookmarks

**Bookmarks** are your own. **My Connections Bookmarks** are folders your
connections have shared with you. A **Private** badge marks bookmarks only you
can see, and a **Chat** badge marks bookmarked chatrooms.
